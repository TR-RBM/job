# Explicit namespaces and mount policies

## What it does

Provides optional launch controls for the namespaces a Job enters (`user`, `mount`, `pid`, `ipc`, `uts`, `cgroup`), a read-only root, a private `/tmp` and `/var/tmp`, and paths that stay writable under a read-only root. Nothing is implied: mount policies are refused unless `mount` and `user` are both requested, and `pid` is refused without `mount`. With `pid`, `pidns.rs` forks the init of the namespace inside the launch: it is number 1, mounts a fresh `/proc`, forks the command, reaps orphans, forwards signals sent to it from inside, and reports the command's wait status to the supervisor over a socket pair; the supervisor's side (`Init`) adopts it, takes the status, and kills it after the leftover processes are recorded. Typed values resolve Queue/Group and pinned-profile defaults with independently tracked origins. Paths, maps and the parent's namespace inodes are prepared before fork; the workload child applies the steps without allocation, after the network steps and before Landlock, process and security controls, and reads back namespaces, the read-only flag and the private tmp before exec. A step that fails names itself in the start error. Remote work validates and applies on the execution host. Launch results keep the applied settings, and `capabilities()` reports what the host offers, including the network modes.

## How to test

Run the isolation_policy_ service integration cases through job. They cover the host matrix in JSON and text, requested against unrequested namespaces, a user namespace shared with `--net none`, a read-only root with writable paths and a private tmp, refusals before the payload and at launch, Queue defaults, `inherit`, `unset`, a profile, retry and restart, the remote transport staying in the service's namespaces, and coexistence with seccomp, capability reduction and an rlimit. State migration cases isolation_launch_controls_survive_archival_backup_and_reject_invalid_outcomes and store_without_isolation_fields_loads_unchanged cover storage. The pidns_ cases cover the PID namespace: in `watch_backend.rs` the namespace and `/proc` readback, exit status and fatal signal, leftover processes, `job signal`, cancel, orphan reaping, mount, security, process and network controls, refusals, defaults and retry, a daemon restart, a nested client and the remote transport; in `terminal_sessions.rs` job control under `--pty`; in `durability.rs` the launch gate and a supervisor killed during cleanup; in `cli_contract.rs` `run --stdin` and the two streams; in `freezer.rs` (delegated cgroup) suspend and continue, leftovers and cancel from the cgroup. No new unit tests.
